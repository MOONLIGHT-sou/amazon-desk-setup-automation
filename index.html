Amazon Desk Setup

Better desks. Better work.

Curated desk-setup ideas, product research, and content built for students, creators, gamers, and work-from-home users.

What this project does

Amazon Desk Setup is a content and automation project that researches desk-setup products, verifies product identity and evidence, applies quality and safety checks, and prepares content for Pinterest and Medium.

Publishing is intentionally protected by authorization and validation gates. Automatic product selection does not by itself authorize publication.

Pinterest integration

When authorized by the account owner, the application is intended to use Pinterest's API to create and manage organic Pins for the owner's account. It does not request or store a Pinterest password.

Amazon affiliate content

Some published content may contain Amazon affiliate links. Product availability, prices, and other commercial information can change on Amazon.
